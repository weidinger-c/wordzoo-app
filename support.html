Common questions

I bought Remove Ads but WordZoo still offers me videos.

That's intended. Remove Ads permanently ends the full-screen interstitials between levels and doubles the coins you earn for finishing a level. The optional “Free hint” and “Double coins” videos stay available as bonuses you can take if you want them — nothing plays unless you tap it.

My purchase isn't unlocked, or I have a new iPhone.

Open the shop and tap Restore Purchases, making sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. Hint packs are consumable and are spent as you use them, so only Remove Ads is restored. If it still doesn't unlock, email me.

WordZoo paused and asks me to watch an ad or reconnect.

WordZoo is free because of its ads. If several ads in a row couldn't be shown — usually because the device is offline or an ad blocker is active — the game asks you to reconnect and watch one before continuing. Connecting to the internet and tapping Reconnect & Try Again fixes it; with Remove Ads this never happens.

How do I change my choice about personalized ads?

Open WordZoo's Settings. Where consent applies (the EEA, UK and Switzerland) there's a Privacy options row that reopens Google's consent form so you can review or withdraw your choices. Apple's separate tracking permission lives in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.

WordZoo didn't accept a word I know is real.

Each level has its own target words, and anything else valid counts as a bonus word. Bonus words are checked against the dictionary built into iOS, which covers contemporary vocabulary — so archaic spellings, names, and abbreviations often miss. Send me the word and the level and I'll take a look.

Where is my progress saved? Could I lose it?

On your iPhone, and — if you're signed in to iCloud — also in your own iCloud, which keeps it in sync across your Apple devices and brings it back after reinstalling. WordZoo has no accounts and no server. If iCloud is turned off for WordZoo, deleting the app erases your animals and progress. Purchases live with your Apple Account and can be restored afterwards.

How do coins, hints and keeper meals work?

Solving target words and finishing levels earns coins; bonus words earn treats too. A hint reveals one letter, and three hints let the keeper radio solve a whole word. In the shop you can trade 100 coins for a hint, buy hint packs, or watch an optional video for a free one. Reaching milestones in a chapter prepares keeper meals — feed them to your rescued animal at the keeper station to help it grow through its four life stages.

What are the Daily Keeper Rounds and the streak?

Each day brings five puzzles picked from that date, so everyone gets the same set. Finishing all five awards bonus coins and advances your streak. There are no timers — you can take as long as you like.

Which languages can I play in?

WordZoo is available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch, each with its own puzzles, and follows your device language by default. You can override it in Settings › Language; restart WordZoo afterwards for the change to fully take effect.

Can I turn the music and sounds off?

Settings has separate switches and volume sliders for background music and sound effects. WordZoo follows your silent switch and mixes with your own music or podcasts instead of interrupting them.

I'd like a refund.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by WordZoo. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account you purchased with.
